About NAGA Esports

Founded in year 2021, Naga Esports Association has a strong will to form a platform for creative minds to cultivate their talents and showcase it to the rest of the world. Our passion is to help create a community for esports enthusiast where they can learn and grow, and offer a space for recreation, cultural and sporting activities. At NEA, we offer a place for gaming enthusiast to help them bring out the best of their abilities, and create more opportunities for them to display their talent through competitions.

Presidents Messeage

As the President of the Naga Esports Association, I am happy to lead the state’s gamers as the state is slowly gaining momentum in the esports scene with so much potential amongst them. Games such as Pubg, COD, Valorant, Dota, etc., have implemented themselves into the gaming scene and has gained popularity among the community with thousands of youths gathering together to battle it out.

Esports in Nagaland

Although gaming as a profession has not fully been accepted as a proper career in the state, with state of the art infrastructure and support, Nagaland can see itself moving up in the gaming community and might be on par with other professional gamers from countries which produce the best esports teams such as Korea, and Malaysia. A team called The Zerg was also formed and qualified for the Pubg Mobile India tournament and made it to the top 16, securing themselves a spot in the tournament. Not only forming proper teams, but with streaming gaining popularity worldwide, there are gamers who stream themselves gaming on platforms such as Twitch or Youtube, and earn a good amount of revenue. A good, proper gamer can amass a good fanbase and this allows for them to gain recognition worldwide. Mobile games have also taken the community by storm and have become easily accessible by anyone with an interest in the gaming world. The age of internet has truly helped this generation follow a career which they enjoy, and gives them access to jobs which does not require them to sit at an office desk .
